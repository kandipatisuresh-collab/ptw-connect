## Phase 1 Entry and Exit Rules

Phase 1 is complete only when B-001 evidence is accepted. B-002 and all later rows
remain blocked until the Phase 1 review record contains accepted HSE, operations, security,
legal/compliance, architecture, and customer SAP decisions applicable to the next phase.
